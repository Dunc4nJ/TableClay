## Brand Voice

### Personality
- **Warm & Artisanal** - handmade feel, personal touch
- **Playful but Not Childish** - whimsical (clouds, bows) but sophisticated
- **Accessible** - pottery for everyone, not just experts
- **Genuine** - real reviews, real artisans, real stories

### Tone Guidelines
- **Do:** Sound handmade, personal, intentional, joyful
- **Do:** Emphasize the story behind each piece
- **Do:** Be encouraging about DIY pottery attempts
- **Don't:** Sound mass-produced, corporate, or pushy
- **Don't:** Over-promise or use hyperbole
- **Don't:** Be salesy — let the craft speak

### Words We Use
- Handcrafted, artisan, one-of-a-kind
- Dreamy, whimsical, intentional
- Studio, workshop, maker
- Joy, care, story
- Everyday ritual, functional art

### Words We Avoid
- Cheap, deal, bargain, discount
- Mass-produced, factory
- Perfect (imperfection is part of handmade charm)
- Limited time only, act now (avoid false urgency)
